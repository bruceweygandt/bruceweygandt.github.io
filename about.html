About

I'm Bruce. I work at Paramify as a Forward Deployed Engineer, on the team since November 2025 and full-time in this role since September 2026.

Engineering, about two-thirdsClient advisory, about one-third

On the engineering side I build evidence fetchers in Python, write the regex validators that turn their JSON into pass or fail, and build internal tools on the Claude API. On the advisory side I work with clients on readiness and evidence strategy, alongside 3PAOs and the FedRAMP PMO.

I came to security from translation, and the two are closer than they sound. A requirement written for one audience has to become exact for another. In compliance, that means a control statement an assessor will accept, and the API call that proves it.
